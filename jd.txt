Data Scientist

We are looking for a Data Scientist to join our analytics team. The ideal candidate
will have strong experience building machine learning models, analyzing large
datasets, and communicating insights to stakeholders.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, or a related field
- 2+ years of experience in a data science or machine learning role
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL
- Hands-on experience with machine learning and deep learning frameworks
  such as scikit-learn, TensorFlow, or PyTorch
- Experience with data manipulation libraries such as Pandas and NumPy
- Familiarity with natural language processing (NLP) techniques
- Experience with version control (Git) and cloud platforms (AWS or Azure)
- Ability to visualize and communicate findings using tools like Power BI or Excel
- Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail

Responsibilities:
- Build and evaluate predictive models using machine learning techniques
- Clean, process, and analyze large structured and unstructured datasets
- Collaborate with engineering teams to deploy models into production
- Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders
- Stay current with the latest developments in machine learning and AI
